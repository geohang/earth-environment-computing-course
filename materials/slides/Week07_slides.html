Anatomy

Figure and Axes Vocabulary

  • fig, ax = plt.subplots(): the canvas and the plot area
  • Methods live on ax: ax.plot, ax.set_xlabel, ax.legend
  • Multi-panel: plt.subplots(2, 1, sharex=True)

Earth & Environmental Computing | Week 07 | 5
The Checklist

Non-negotiable Elements

Labels + units

Discharge (cfs). A figure without units cannot be checked.

Readable size

Text legible from the back row, or in a two-column PDF.

Legend

Whenever two lines share a panel. No guessing.

Stated message

Title or caption says what to see, not just what it is.

This checklist is the figure rubric for every remaining lab.

Find Five Problems

fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(3, 2))
ax.plot(stream["date"], stream["discharge_cfs"])
ax.plot(weather["date"], weather["precipitation_mm"])
plt.show()
  • No labels, no units, no legend
  • Two incompatible quantities on one axis
  • Tiny canvas, unreadable ticks, no message

Design Pattern

The Two-Panel Rain-and-River Story

Why two panels

  • Rain in mm/day, flow in cfs: different units
  • Shared x-axis keeps cause above effect
  • Inverted rain axis: hydrologic convention

The highlight

  • axvspan marks the flood window
  • One highlight = one message
  • Annotate the peak, nothing else

Earth & Environmental Computing | Week 07 | 9
Scales

When Linear Axes Lie

ax.semilogy(exceedance * 100, discharge_cfs)
Iowa River flow: ~100 to ~40,000 cfs
Two orders of magnitude on one axis
Linear: everything below floods flattens
Log: low flows and floods both visible
  • The flow-duration curve is meaningful only with a log flow axis
Earth & Environmental Computing | Week 07 | 10
Color

Color Rules That Survive Printing

  • Sequential colormaps for magnitude (viridis, YlGnBu)
  • Never rainbow for quantitative data: it invents boundaries
  • Check once in grayscale before submitting
  • Meaningful color deserves a colorbar with units

Captions

The Three-Part Caption

What

Daily discharge of the Iowa River at Iowa City for 2024, with daily precipitation inverted above.

How

USGS station 05454500 and Iowa Environmental Mesonet data; highlighted band marks the spring flood.

Notice

The river peaked N days after the heaviest rain; baseflow stayed elevated for weeks.

Captions

From Caption to 200-Word Interpretation

  • Caveat: one honest limitation of data or method
  • Group Lab 1 grades all three, not just the picture

Pitfalls

Where Group Figures Go Wrong
