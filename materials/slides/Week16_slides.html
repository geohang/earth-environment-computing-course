What Transfers

The Durable Habits

Verification

Tiny tests, magnitude anchors, two routes to every important number.

Honest scoring

Residuals as diagnosis; unseen data as the judge; calibrated verbs.

Disclosure

Sources cited, assistance logged, work explainable line by line.

Presentations

The Ten-Minute Shape That Works

  • One slide: question and why it matters (1 min)
  • One slide: data and method (2 min)
  • Two or three figures, one claim each (5 min)
  • One slide: limitations and what you would do next (2 min)

Closing

The Course's Quiet Thesis

Claim

Computation is how modern Earth science asks whether its own claims are true.

Evidence

Sixteen weeks of checks: asserts, anchors, residuals, holdouts, logs.

Charge

A reproducible analysis is an argument anyone can check. Hold your own work to it first.
