Earth & Environmental Computing | Week 13 | 3
The Question

Workable vs Career-Sized

Workable

How did the 2024 flood's rain-to-peak lag compare with the five largest events of 2015-2025? Specific quantity, dataset in hand, one method.

A career

How does climate change affect Iowa rivers? No dataset settles it; no method is THE method; the deadline arrives first.

Earth & Environmental Computing | Week 13 | 4
The Question

Three Tests for Any Candidate Question

1

Data in hand?

In the repo, or one approved download away. This week, not week 15.

2

One method?

A single course method answers it, done well.

3

Can it fail?

A question that cannot fail is a description, not a question.

Earth & Environmental Computing | Week 13 | 5
Pause & Think

Scope Check, Live

"We will analyze water quality in Iowa." Run the three tests on it. What is the smallest fix that makes it workable?

Fails all three as stated
Fix: one site, one characteristic, one comparison, e.g. seasonal nitrate 1970s vs 2000s

Worked Example

One Good Project, Sketched

Question

Do smaller watersheds respond faster? Rain-to-peak lag vs drainage area across 17 stations.

Method

Event detection (Lab 3) + time-series lags (Lab 6) + one regression (GL2).

Figures

Lag vs area scatter with fit; two contrasting event hydrographs; station map.

Lab session

How the Proposal Workshop Runs

  • Arrive with a drafted question; leave with a schedule
  • Template: question, data, method, figures, roles, AI plan
  • 10-minute scope check per group with the instructor
  • The most common intervention: shrink the question by half
